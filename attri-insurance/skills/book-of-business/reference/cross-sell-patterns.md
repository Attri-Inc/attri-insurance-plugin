# Cross-Sell Patterns — book-of-business

Common bundle patterns to check when scanning for account-rounding
opportunities. These are starting points, not rules — actual appetite
depends on the carriers the agency represents.

| Existing line | Common missing line to flag |
|---|---|
| Commercial Auto (fleet) | Umbrella/excess liability, Workers Comp |
| General Liability (contractor) | Commercial Auto, Builders Risk, Workers Comp |
| Homeowners | Personal Auto, Umbrella |
| Personal Auto | Homeowners/Renters, Umbrella |
| BOP (small commercial) | Cyber liability, Workers Comp, Umbrella |
| Workers Comp | GL (if not already bundled), EPLI |

## Prioritization

Weight cross-sell flags by:
1. **Premium potential** of the missing line, scaled to account size
2. **Account tenure** — longer-tenured accounts are lower-friction conversations
3. **Recent claim activity** — an account with a recent claim on one line may be a harder cross-sell conversation right now; note this rather than skipping it silently
