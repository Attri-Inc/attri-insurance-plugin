# Thresholds — agency-monday-brief

Red/yellow/green cutoffs for each section. These are starting defaults —
edit them to match the agency's actual book size and targets.

## Renewal exposure (next 30 days)

- 🟢 No renewals flagged (no non-renewal notices, no missing loss runs)
- 🟡 1–2 renewals flagged
- 🔴 3+ renewals flagged, or any flagged renewal inside 15 days of expiration

## Submission pipeline

- 🟢 Pipeline premium ≥ monthly new-business target  # TODO: confirm with owner
- 🟡 50–99% of target
- 🔴 < 50% of target

## Receivables aging

- 🟢 < 5% of receivables over 60 days
- 🟡 5–15% over 60 days
- 🔴 > 15% over 60 days

## Claims

- 🟢 No reopened or escalated claims in the window
- 🟡 1 reopened/escalated claim
- 🔴 2+ reopened/escalated claims, or any claim with no status update in 14+ days
