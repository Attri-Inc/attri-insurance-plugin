# Worked example — FNOL intake with policy found

**Policyholder email (forwarded):** "Hi, a tree branch came down on our
garage roof during last night's storm. No one was hurt but there's a hole
and some water got in. Policy number is HO-4471829."

**Output (abridged):**

- Classified as: **FNOL** (property damage, no injuries, active water intrusion — flagged urgent)
- Policy found: HO-4471829, Homeowners, [Carrier], effective 3/1–3/1, dwelling limit $410,000
- Draft acknowledgment reply: calm, confirms receipt, explains next step is same-day carrier submission, advises policyholder to take photos and mitigate further water damage if safe to do so
- Staged for carrier: date/time of loss (last night, during storm), location (garage roof), description (tree branch, water intrusion), no injuries reported, no other parties involved

Claude then asks: *"Submit FNOL for [policyholder] (HO-4471829) to [Carrier] claims? Reply Y to proceed."*
